# Third-party media in the demo film

Every picture of a cat in the film is a frame or an excerpt of these Wikimedia Commons videos (cut, cropped to 16:9, colour-graded by the film's look). Nothing else from a third party appears on screen: the ad is a fictional brand whose pictures were made with Muse Image, the interface and the bridges are the product's own output, and the textures are generated noise.

## Voice and fonts

- Narration: an AI voice made with ElevenLabs ("Zach - Clear, Natural, Calming"), allowed for the film's narration by the organizers. The product itself calls no voice model outside Meta.
- Fonts: Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif, Figtree, Bricolage Grotesque, Anton, VT323 (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), and the system font of the computer that renders the film.
