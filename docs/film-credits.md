# Third-party media in the demo film

The film's cats are photos from Wikimedia Commons and videos from Pexels. Nothing else from a third party appears on screen: the ad is a fictional brand (pictures made with Muse Image, animated with Gemini Omni outside the product, labelled as AI-generated), the interface and the bridges are the product's own output, the textures are generated noise.

## Voice and fonts

- Fonts: Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif, Figtree, Bricolage Grotesque, Anton, VT323 (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts).
